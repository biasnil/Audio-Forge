# Privacy

AudioForge is a music player that works on your computer. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app reads

The music folders you add, and the files you open.

## What stays on your computer

Your settings, playlists and smart playlists, queue, play counts and loved songs, library cache, Key/BPM analysis, detected genres, video wallpaper and visualizer choices and the warnings log, in `%APPDATA%\AudioForge`, and the lyrics cache in your temporary files folder. Your Musixmatch API key is encrypted with Windows, so only your Windows account can read it.

Uninstalling AudioForge keeps this folder, so a reinstall keeps your library and playlists. Delete the folder to remove it all.

## What leaves your computer, and only when needed

**LRCLIB** (lrclib.net): to find lyrics, the song's title and artist.

**Musixmatch**: the song's title and artist, and your API key. Only if you added a key.

**MusicBrainz** (musicbrainz.org): the song's title and artist, when you use Tag Music > MusicBrainz or Fill from internet.

**GitHub** (api.github.com): one request for the list of AudioForge releases, to tell you about updates. Only when you click Version in Settings, or turn on Check for Updates Automatically (off unless you turn it on).

**Discord**: if you turn on Rich Presence, the song's title, artist and playing time go to the Discord app on your computer, which shows them on your Discord profile.

Genre detection runs entirely on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

These services see your computer's IP address, as any website you visit does. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Report a Problem

Report a Problem opens an email in your email app with the app version, Windows version and language filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

## Your files

The app changes a song's tags or cover only when you save, and moves a file to the Recycle Bin only after you confirm.
