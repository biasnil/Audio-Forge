# Privacy

AudioForge is a music player that works on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

# What the app reads

The music library on your phone, if you allow it, and the folders you add yourself.

# What stays on your phone

Your settings, playlists, play counts and loved songs, the lyrics cache, your podcast subscriptions, your listening history (for Listening stats), Key/BPM analysis and genre detection results, and your Musixmatch API key, which is encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of this. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's settings and playlists in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. The Musixmatch key can't be restored from a backup and has to be entered again.

# What leaves your phone, and only when needed

LRCLIB (lrclib.net): to find lyrics, the song's title and artist, or a search made from the file name.

Musixmatch: the song's title and artist, and your API key. Only if you added a key.

GitHub (api.github.com): one request for the number of the latest AudioForge release, to tell you about updates. You can turn this off in Settings, under About.

Apple (itunes.apple.com): the words you type when you search for podcasts. Show artwork in the results is loaded from Apple's servers.

Podcast hosts: when you open a show, check for new episodes or download an episode, the show's feed, artwork and audio are fetched from the servers the show uses. Downloads are made by Android's download manager and saved in your phone's Podcasts folder.

These services see your phone's IP address, as any website you visit does. Their own privacy policies apply.

Genre detection runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

# Your files

The app changes a song's tags or cover only when you tap Save, and deletes a song only after you confirm.
